Lemon Law Intake Qualifier

A document-first intake pipeline for California lemon law practices. It reads the stack of dealership repair orders a firm already has, screens the case against Song-Beverly and Tanner Act criteria, and drafts an attorney-ready case summary — with a human review gate before anything reaches a client or the case file.

How it works

Stage 01

Repair-order extraction

Reads repair orders and pulls dates, mileage, complaint, and repair-attempt data into a structured record. Low-confidence fields and document conflicts are flagged for a person to verify, not silently accepted.

Stage 02

Statutory screening

Runs the extracted case against Civ. Code § 1793.2 and the § 1793.22 presumption, with every threshold held in one attorney-owned config file, tagged "verify with counsel."

Stage 03

Attorney case summary

Drafts a case-value estimate, procedural track, and a plain-English intake summary. Nothing leaves draft status, and nothing books a consultation, until an attorney marks it reviewed.
